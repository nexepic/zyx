## Fixed tests retain independent negative and efficiency coverage

Five former refusal thresholds mixed particular container layouts with logical payload estimates. They now test explicit ownership/lower bounds and keep separately reviewable success ceilings; production quotas are not increased.

- Administration retains the original 4 KiB success ceiling. After the administrative source exists, sibling parent pressure leaves less than one public result row's backing slot. Publication must fail without mutation, refund its account, and retry successfully after the sibling releases. The former `1024 alone must fail` is no longer treated as a universal STL layout fact.
- Selected-column scalar output (`size(body)`) retains its original success ceiling and sorted-read order. A separate genuinely owning three-string projection rejects below its 3000-byte raw payload lower bound, then verifies contents/order/resource ownership at an independent fixed 16 KiB ceiling, with complete refund after result destruction. The 16 KiB case is an added workload, not a raised scalar ceiling.
- Dijkstra independently rejects below the owning three-result 18000-byte payload lower bound and retains the existing 64 KiB success ceiling/account refund. A destroyed source path is not charged solely to keep the old 20 KiB refusal green.
- TopK uses 1000 hits so its candidate-working-set distinction is real. It adds only the exact hit-vector input backing allowance; its non-input success workspace remains the former ten-hit working allowance. Less than one logical row is independently insufficient, and a full sort on the same larger input/cap must refuse while bounded TopK succeeds.
- CSV keeps the original 24 KiB successful ceiling. At property source admission the test checks live CSV charge is at least the independent source charge plus its 2000-byte header. Sibling pressure leaves 5999 bytes, less than the selected 6000-byte payload. It verifies zero payload-copy reads/zero delivery on refusal, complete query refund, unchanged sibling charge and successful same-cap retry with owned output/refund. Other low local CSV budget tests remain unchanged.
