## Additional design record received during audit

The handoff describes separate physical generation, durable graph logical revision, schema revision, and owner revision to reject ABA. Same-value no-ops, rollback, configuration-only changes and ID leases must not advance graph revision. Supported concurrency is one writable owner with multiple staged transactions on the same backend and serialized publication, not independent multiprocess writers or general serializability. Ordinary or exposed reads use a conservative whole-graph fence; limited blind writes check bounded owner dependencies.

The described final PROPERTY directory design stores a 64-bit owner revision in the constrained-zero upper four bytes of the slot generation and the four free-bytes bytes of its 32-byte record, retaining a 32-bit slot generation and 126 records per 4096-byte page. It covered empty-property tombstones, old snapshots, other owners on the same page, deletion/recreation, rollback/reopen, ROW/column conversions in both directions, atomic version/data publication, and separation of capacity from graph change. Only the latest storage format is required; final format version remains unknown. Do not reinstate old codecs, migrations, fallbacks or experimental gates. Historical final-sync fault tests showed reopen observes either all-old or all-new data and version; they did not establish power-loss durability.
