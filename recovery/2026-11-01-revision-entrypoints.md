## Supported logical entrypoints and internal physical primitives

Production callsite audit at 5ee56f33: `upsertEntityPageLocations`, `upsertEntityPropertyLocations`, `upsertVectorLocations`, and `upsertVectorManifests` have **zero** direct `src/storage/relocatable` callers outside their definitions. `replaceEntityPageInExistingPool` has three EntityPageStore callsites (typed logical writers); `upsertIndexRoots` has one BTreePageStore raw-root caller; `retireVectorManifestPageInExistingPool` has one VectorManifestStore raw-retirement caller; `materializeAndPublishTreeAcrossPools` has one BulkBTreeBuilder caller. The backend's metadata handle is private. Additional raw vector/manifest calls include `OfflineVectorAdjacencyRebuild` manifest replacement and `VectorIndexStateReclaimer` manifest retirement; those are physical derived maintenance. `BulkBTreeBuilder::build` has four vector mapping adapters and one offline scalar derived-index materializer, while online schema construction uses a staged transaction plan.

The supported standalone logical interfaces are EntityPageStore typed writes and graph batches; BTreePageStore scalar upsert/erase; NativeGraphWriteTransaction entity/property/scalar/vector operations; schema sessions; and backend logical graph/streamed edge writers. Their logical intents are derived or explicitly supplied as documented above. Raw page bytes, typed routes, manifest replacement and bulk physical tree construction are physical primitives rather than complete graph transactions; their callers must explicitly declare logical publication when using them to carry semantic change, including exact Property owners. This is a contract boundary, not evidence that arbitrary raw byte mutation is safe concurrent with staged graph transactions. Physical vector adjacency rebuild and dropped-index reclamation explicitly clear graph intent.

The revision-fence change under test replaces physical graph/data root equality with durable graph and schema revision equality. Planner rebase still validates identity, generation/LSN progress, nonzero revisions and monotonic/exhausted node/edge ID high-water marks. Final typed candidate validation remains in force. Do not treat the untested source checkpoint as complete until default/mixed/SCAN and the known regressions pass.
