Date: 2026-10-14. Status: production-runtime selection bitmap budget migration passes selected verification; complete S2 remains unaccepted. Accepted progress stays 5/34 (14.7%), with no newly accepted item.

SelectionVector now uses a PMR vector. Five query-runtime bitmap allocations use BudgetMemoryResource against their shared MaterializedByteBudget, admitting actual allocator requests before upstream allocation and refunding capacity on deallocation. Separate manual bitmap reservations are removed to prevent duplicate accounting. requiredStorageBytes remains a planning estimate; storageBytes reports capacity. Copy construction preserves the source allocator, assignment preserves the destination allocator, and cross-resource movement allocates successfully before publishing into the destination. Retained source capacity stays charged until destruction. Failed allocation preserves selections and refunds unsuccessful admission. Resources must outlive all bitmap owners and their copies.

The five consumers are property prefetch, direct Edge COUNT, direct Edge selected-property reading, optional NodeScan predicate selection, and adjacency selected-property traversal. Optional NodeScan can decline bitmap admission only before filtering or observable effects. bad_alloc, cancellation, and corruption propagate.
